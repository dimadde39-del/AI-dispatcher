# Wiki Index

This wiki is maintained by agents. It summarizes raw documents, decisions, concepts, and operating knowledge for AI Dispatcher.

The wiki should summarize raw documents, not replace them. Canonical uploaded sources remain in `knowledge/raw/`.

## Sections

- `entities/`: important product and domain objects.
- `concepts/`: reusable ideas, policies, and business rules.
- `sources/`: summaries of raw source documents.
- `synthesis/`: cross-source conclusions and recommendations.
- `log.md`: operation log for meaningful wiki updates.
