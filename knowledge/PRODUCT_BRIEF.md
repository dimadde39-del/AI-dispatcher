# Product Brief

## Positioning

We sell saved orders, not “AI bot”.

AI Dispatcher sells saved orders, not an "AI bot".

The product helps individual field service masters and small teams in Kazakhstan capture leads they miss while working, driving, or speaking with another client.

## Main ICPs

- Plumber.
- Washing machine repair master.
- Fridge repair master.
- Electrician.
- Locksmith.
- Conditioner installer.

## Core Flow

missed call -> forwarding -> AI dispatcher -> lead extraction -> Telegram card -> master accepts -> value report

1. Client calls a master.
2. Master misses the call or is busy.
3. Call forwarding sends the call to the AI dispatcher.
4. AI dispatcher asks for the client's problem, address or district, urgency, and name.
5. AI extracts the lead.
6. Telegram sends a lead card to the master.
7. Master accepts the lead.
8. Product reports value through captured and accepted missed-call leads.

## Main KPI

Accepted leads captured from missed calls.

## Product Promise

The master should feel that AI Dispatcher quietly protects revenue while they are busy. The product must make saved orders visible and easy to act on.

Raw source framing: the strongest demo is not "AI talked"; it is "this order would have gone to the next OLX listing, and now the master has a qualified Telegram lead."

## Language Support Warning

MVP voice behavior is Russian-first. Do not claim reliable Kazakh support yet; KZ-only and mixed
RU/KZ calls are callback-required fallback until a provider benchmark proves production quality.

## Non-Goals For Now

- Customer-facing marketplace.
- Public landing architecture.
- Mobile app.
- Billing provider integration.
- Public customer accounts.
- WhatsApp/SMS client notifications.
- Self-host voice stack.
